A Note from the Secretary-General

Welcome, Delegates

Greetings! My name is Megha, and I am beyond honoured to be serving as Secretary General of JCMUN 2026.

This year's theme, Finding Common Ground: Rebuilding Peace in a Divided World, could not feel more relevant. We are living in a time where division is loud and cooperation feels harder than ever, and I genuinely believe that what happens in a MUN committee room is one small but meaningful answer to that. Delegates are learning to listen, to negotiate, to find shared solutions with people they disagree with, which is what the world needs more of right now.

The JCMUN Secretariat and team eagerly looks forward to welcoming you in November.

Megha Thaleshwar Secretary-General, JCMUN 2026
