Our Story

A Tradition of Diplomacy

Jumeirah College Model United Nations (JCMUN) began in the 2023–2024 academic year with a simple ambition: to provide students with a platform to engage in global affairs, develop their diplomatic skills, and experience international relations through debate and negotiation.

Our inaugural conference welcomed just 60 delegates. Despite a few hiccups and organisational challenges, the dedication of our students and staff ensured that the event was successfully delivered, laying the foundations for future conferences.

The second edition, held in 2024–2025, marked a significant step forward, welcoming 250 delegates from a wider range of schools. The conference featured engaging debates, lively discussions, and meaningful diplomatic negotiations, making it a tremendous success.

Our third edition, in 2025–2026, exceeded all expectations, bringing together close to 400 attendees. With greater participation, diverse perspectives, and excellent committee debates, it became a landmark event for Jumeirah College.

From our first conference to our third, JCMUN has become much more than an annual event. It is a testament to the hard work of our students and staff and their shared passion for diplomacy, leadership, and international affairs. We look forward to building on this success and seeing what the future holds for JCMUN.

Our Mission

JCMUN brings students together to debate, negotiate and collaborate on the world’s most pressing issues. Through a rigorous, inclusive and welcoming conference, we develop confident speakers, critical thinkers and principled leaders.

Our Vision

To be a leading student conference in the region, where young people shape informed, respectful and constructive dialogue on global affairs.

Our Host School

Jumeirah College is a GEMS secondary school with a sixth form, delivering the National Curriculum for England through a full range of GCSE and A Level courses. Its international community includes over 1,100 students from more than 59 nationalities, and inspectors have repeatedly rated it Outstanding. Located in Al Safa, Dubai, the college is the proud host of JCMUN. It welcomes delegates to its campus and supports the conference throughout, and its global outlook reflects the spirit of the event.
