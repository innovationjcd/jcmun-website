Know Before You Go

Dress Code & Logistics

Dress Code

Delegates are expected to wear western business attire throughout the conference: suits, blazers, dress shirts, ties, formal dresses or skirts of appropriate length, and formal shoes. National dress is welcome where appropriate. Jeans, sneakers, and casual wear are not permitted in committee sessions.

What to Bring

  • Printed or digital copy of your position paper and research
  • Laptop or tablet for unmoderated caucuses (per committee rules)
  • School ID or Emirates ID for check-in
  • Reusable water bottle

Venue & Logistics

JCMUN 4 takes place at Jumeirah College, Street 19, Al Safa 1, Dubai, UAE. Delegate registration opens at 2:00 PM on Friday, November 20. Lunch is provided on Saturday and Sunday.
