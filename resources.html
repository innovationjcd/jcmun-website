Know Before You Go

Dress Code & Logistics

Dress Code

Delegates are expected to wear western business attire throughout the conference: suits, blazers, dress shirts, ties, formal dresses or skirts of appropriate length, and formal shoes. National dress is welcome where appropriate. Jeans, sneakers, and casual wear are not permitted in committee sessions. For examples, see this Model UN dress code guide.

First Time at MUN?

Feeling nervous? Almost everyone is. Every delegate in the room had a first conference once, and plenty of the people sitting next to you are on their first one too. You don’t need to know every rule or have the perfect speech. Read your background guide, get to know your country, and put your placard up even when your heart is racing. Our chairs and Secretariat are here to help, so ask questions, make friends and enjoy it.

Want a head start? These are all free:

  • 5 Ways a First-Timer Can Prepare for a MUN Conference. Covers researching your country and getting help with rules of procedure and the background guide.
  • Best Delegate Resource Library. Guides for opening speeches, position papers and resolutions.
  • Diplomat Guide for MUN Beginners. A step-by-step plan for your first conference, plus a six-week prep checklist. It asks you to fill in a short form first.
